🌟 Why Choose iCleaners?

iCleaners is an enterprise-grade, multi-store Laundry and Dry Cleaning ERP built specifically to eliminate the daily operational bottlenecks of laundry businesses. While generic POS systems struggle with garment identification and continuous roll printing, iCleaners features an integrated Silent Printing Server. Cashiers can process orders and instantly print 80mm / 58mm thermal receipts and waterproof 75mm / 50mm garment cloth wash tags with Code128 barcodes — completely bypassing the browser's slow and disruptive Ctrl+P print dialog!

Whether running a single neighborhood dry cleaner or expanding a multi-branch franchise network across different cities, iCleaners delivers complete control with real-time financial ledgers, franchisee commission tracking, multi-currency support, customer advance accounts, and full localization in 10 languages (including native RTL for Arabic).

🚀 Core Modules & Features

1. High-Speed Interactive Point of Sale (POS)

  • Single-Screen Checkout: Select store, search customers, choose garments, apply coupon codes, and select payment gateways on one unified screen.
  • Garment Inspection & Notes: Add notes per garment (stains, damages, special care instructions) that print directly on wash tags.
  • Dynamic Add-ons & Surcharges: Support for express delivery, starching, perfume wash, and custom service modifiers.
  • Instant Customer Modal: Register new clients on the fly without abandoning current cart items.
  • Barcode Scanner Integration: Compatible with standard USB and Bluetooth barcode scanners for fast order retrieval.

2. Dual-Mode Silent Hardware Printing System (Exclusive Feature!)

  • Silent Desktop Printing Bridge (Port 4321): Connects browser POS directly to the OS print spooler with zero popups or confirmation dialogues.
  • Thermal Receipt Printing (80mm & 58mm): Itemized customer bill, tax breakdown, barcode, store logo, customizable footer, and automatic cash drawer kick.
  • Garment Wash Tag Printing (75mm & 50mm): Prints waterproof nylon/cloth tags per individual garment piece with Code128 barcodes, customer name, rack number, and item sequence (e.g. ORD-1001-01).
  • Native Browser Fallback: If the printing server is offline, the POS automatically falls back to standard browser printing (window.print()) with zero downtime.

3. Multi-Store & Franchise Multi-Branch Management

  • Central Master Administration: Monitor aggregate revenue, order volume, and active branches from a single unified command center.
  • Branch-Level Isolation: Store Managers and Cashiers only access their respective store's orders, cash drawer, inventory, and customers.
  • Franchise Commission System: Configure custom commission percentage rates per branch with automated payout ledger calculations.
  • Store Self-Registration: Enable prospective branch owners to apply online with master admin approval workflows.

4. Financial Accounting, Cash Management & Reports

  • Customer Ledger & Advance Accounts: Track customer debit/credit balances, prepaid wallet deposits, and payment histories.
  • Store Cash Drawers: Manage daily cash in/out, cashier float balances, and register shifts.
  • Executive Reports Suite:
    • Daily Summary Report: Cash vs. Card vs. UPI/Online daily totals.
    • Order Reports: Detailed breakdown filtered by date range, store, delivery status, and payment state.
    • Sales & Revenue Report: Net sales, gross discounts, and delivery surcharges.
    • Tax / VAT Reports: Compliant breakdown of collected VAT/GST per store.
    • Profit & Loss (P&L) Statement: Real-time net margin calculation (Gross Revenue − Store Expenses).
  • 1-Click Excel Export: Export every report to formatted .xlsx spreadsheets using integrated ExcelJS.

5. 10-Language Localization with Native RTL

  • 10 World Languages Included: English, Hindi (हिन्दी), Portuguese (Português), Spanish (Español), French (Français), Chinese (中文), Arabic (العربية - Full RTL), Indonesian (Bahasa), Filipino (Tagalog), Ukrainian (Українська).
  • 100% Dictionary Key Parity: Over 466 translation keys per language with a bulletproof fallback proxy to prevent missing strings.
  • Instant Language Switcher: Switch languages seamlessly from the topbar or login screen with zero page errors.

6. Multi-Tier Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

  • Super / Master Admin: Global store oversight, branch approvals, system settings, payout management.
  • Store Manager: Branch-scoped POS, store orders, expenses, branch staff management.
  • Cashier / Staff: POS checkout, ticket printing, garment tag generation, order status updates.
  • Customer Portal: Online self-service tracking, invoice download, service pricing review.

🖨️ Hardware & Printer Compatibility

  • Thermal Receipt Printers: Epson, Star Micronics, Citizen, Xprinter, Bixolon, Munbyn, POS-58, POS-80 (USB, Ethernet, Wi-Fi).
